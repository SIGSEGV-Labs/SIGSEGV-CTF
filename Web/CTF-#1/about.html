About the county

Local government, built around service

Meridian County has provided civic services to its residents since 1891. Our departments handle everything from land records to road maintenance, working out of the county courthouse and satellite offices across the district.

Our mission

The Meridian County government exists to provide transparent, accessible, and efficient services to every resident — regardless of neighborhood or income. We manage public records, issue permits, maintain county infrastructure, and support elected officials in serving the community.

County offices are staffed Monday through Friday, and most services are also available through this website. Where an online service isn't yet available, our IT department is gradually migrating internal tools to the public portal — a project that has been ongoing since our 2024 systems modernization initiative.
